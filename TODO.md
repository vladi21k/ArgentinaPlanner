## 💵 Cash planning note

You fly into FTE and transfer straight to El Chaltén the same day — **no El Calafate overnight to fall back on**. Withdraw pesos at FTE / El Calafate before leaving, covering 3 nights in El Chaltén plus the gate-paid fees above; El Chaltén's single ATM is unreliable.

**But note the split:** the **El Chaltén trail fees are card/QR only — cash won't work there.** You need *both* pesos in hand for food and shops *and* a working card for the park gates. Buying the trail pass online in advance removes the dependency on signal at the gate.
